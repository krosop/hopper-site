What Hopper does, what it doesn't, and what to expect when a file doesn't cooperate.

Basics

What is Hopper?

A browser extension that detects video on supported pages, downloads it from the web through a small Windows companion, and converts or trims files on your own computer.

How does auto-detect work?

Open a supported video page and click Hopper. The Video tab reads the page you are already on, finds the video, and asks the companion for the available formats. No link hunting, no copy-paste.

Can Hopper download videos from the web?

Yes, on supported video pages. Auto-detect finds the video, the companion fetches the format you choose over your own connection, and it saves to your Downloads folder. The companion is coming soon to the Microsoft Store; local file conversion works without it. Use it only on media you have the right to download and keep.

Is Hopper free?

Yes. There is no account, subscription, or paid tier.

Which browser does it support?

Microsoft Edge on Windows 10 and 11 is the supported target. Other Chromium browsers may load the package, but they are not tested or supported.

Is there a macOS or Linux version?

No. Hopper is built for Windows and Microsoft Edge. The companion is a Windows app.

How do I remove it?

Open edge://extensions, find Hopper, and choose Remove. That deletes its local preferences. Converted files stay in your downloads folder.

Formats and files

Which output formats can I choose?

MP4 and WebM for video; MP3 and M4A for audio. Each has three quality levels: high, balanced, and small.

Can I trim a file?

Yes. Set a start and end time, or line up clips to merge. Times accept seconds or mm:ss.

Where are converted files saved?

Wherever your browser saves downloads. Hopper hands the finished file to the browser's normal download flow and keeps no copy.

Why is the output larger than the source?

Converting between codecs re-encodes the file. A high quality setting can produce a larger file than a highly compressed source. Try the balanced or small setting.

Can Hopper open a file with DRM?

No. Hopper does not bypass access controls or copy protection. Use it on media you have the right to convert or save.

Privacy

Does Hopper upload my files?

No. Local file conversion happens on your computer. Auto-detect and web downloads use the companion, which fetches the page you choose through your own connection.

Does Hopper collect analytics?

No analytics, telemetry, advertising IDs, or tracking pixels. There is nothing to opt out of.

What does the extension store?

Your last output format and quality, in local browser storage. Removing the extension deletes them.

Does it read every page I visit?

No. It reads the active tab's address when you open the Video tab so it can detect the video. It reads page contents only when you click Find media on this page, and only to list direct video and audio links.

Companion

Do I need the companion?

For local file conversion, no. For auto-detect and web downloads, yes. The companion is the Windows app that fetches the media; its Microsoft Store listing is coming soon.

Where do I get the companion?

A Microsoft Store listing is coming soon. It is not offered on this page yet; the extension's local file conversion works without it.

Does the companion send data anywhere?

It contacts the page you choose, exactly as a browser would. The extension reaches the companion over 127.0.0.1, which is your own machine.

Troubleshooting

Why does Edge show a developer-mode warning?

The package installs as an unpacked extension until the Edge Add-ons listing opens. Edge shows that warning for every unpacked extension. It is not specific to Hopper.

A conversion stops partway

Try a shorter trim or a smaller file, and close heavy tabs to free memory. If it still fails, email the file type, size, and the exact message.

The popup won't open

Reload the extension from edge://extensions, then reopen it. If it still won't open, remove it and load the folder again.

How do updates work?

Unpacked extensions do not update themselves. Download the new package, replace the folder, and reload Hopper. Once the Edge Add-ons listing is live, updates will come through the store.

Still have a question?

Email is the support channel. Include your Hopper version and the exact message you saw.
