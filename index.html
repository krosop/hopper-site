Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Short answers to the things people ask before installing.

Is Hopper free?

Yes. There is no account, subscription, or paid tier.

How does auto-detect work?

Open a supported video page and click Hopper. The Video tab reads the page you are already on, finds the video, and asks the companion for the available formats. No link hunting, no copy-paste.

Can Hopper download videos from the web?

Yes, on supported video pages. Open the page, click Hopper, and auto-detect finds the video. The companion fetches the format you choose from the web over your own connection and saves it to Downloads. The companion is coming soon to the Microsoft Store; local file conversion works without it. Use it only on media you have the right to download and keep.

Does Hopper upload my files?

No. Local file conversion happens on your computer. Auto-detect and video downloads use the companion, which fetches the page you choose through your own connection.

Which formats can I choose?

MP4, WebM, MP3, and M4A. MP4 and WebM are video; MP3 and M4A are audio.

Do I need the companion?

For local file conversion, no. For auto-detect and video downloads, yes. The companion is a Windows app; its Microsoft Store listing is coming soon.
